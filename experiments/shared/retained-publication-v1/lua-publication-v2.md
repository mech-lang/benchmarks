The shared transform and guarded driver have an explicit Lua interpreted
admission. It reuses twelve retained subject observations and six matched
Mech observations. It starts no benchmark. The original LuaJIT records and
the other nine Lua workload records remain unchanged.

Each of the four Lua profiles owns two counted files: its application and
the complete shared general GMP adapter. Five distinct physical source
files therefore produce four complete bundles:

| Retained profile | Canonical units | Physical source lines |

| `lua-listed-1` | 280 | 181 |

| `lua-listed-6` | 281 | 182 |
| `lua-listed-7` | 275 | 176 |

The helper contributes 218 canonical units and 119 physical lines to every
bundle. Formatted LOC stays unavailable. All four forms enter source
inventory; the fastest and smallest qualified forms enter the displayed
endpoint pool. Both matched controls remain in every admitted source record,
with the fast control used for its explicit paired comparison. These controls
do not replace the catalog's stored timing baseline.

The Lua aggregate expands from nine to ten measured workloads. This adapter
rejects an unexpected new timing or canonical-LOC minimum. The existing
R, APL and Uiua one-file rules remain unchanged. Lua's audit uses the actual
`source_manifest` reference, including the reviewed archived metadata
exception, rather than inventing a source-audit artifact.
