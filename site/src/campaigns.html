What makes these results comparable?

Comparisons require the same task, source, input schedule, measured boundary, backend and environment. Preparation, managed execution, output ownership and destruction have separate measurements. A changed input is checked against an independent oracle, and a no-execution control verifies that the published answer stays unchanged until a turn runs.

Raw observations are independent process forks. Three forks support exploration; five paired forks support an initial screening interval. Suspected regressions need a separate confirmation campaign. Shared-runner timings never fail CI. Planned coverage makes no claim about runtime support.

Resident turns are diagnostic measurements. The language comparison preserves its own recorded whole-process and interpreted boundaries. Managed admission counters and process RSS measure different things.
